Madoox Regime

Discipline, made routine.

What it is. Madoox Regime helps you build and hold daily habits through a simple task routine and a visible streak, so discipline becomes something you can see, not just feel.
Security. Sign in with your account, or unlock instantly with fingerprint authentication for faster, safer access.
Philosophy. Small, repeated actions compound. The regime is not about intensity, it is about showing up.
Appearance. Pick an accent color for your regime. This is saved to your device and account.
